# The appoggiatura: the leaning note

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. VI §§73–74, pp. 22–26. Original prose.

An **appoggiatura** is a small note written just before a main note.
Its name means "leaning": it falls on the beat, takes the stress, and
borrows real time from the main note, which then follows softly.
Gehrkens gives three rules for how much time it takes: half the value
of a plain note, two thirds of a dotted note, and — before a note tied
to another — the whole value of the first of the two.

# The turn: circling the note

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. VI §§69–71, pp. 22–26. Original prose.

The **turn** sign stands for four notes that circle the written one:
the note above, the note itself, the note below, and the note again.
Over a short note in a quick tempo the four notes are equal and begin at
once. Written a little to the right of the note, the turn waits: the
note is held first, and the four quick notes come at the very end, just
before the next note. Gehrkens adds that an accidental written above the
sign alters the upper note of the turn, and one written below alters the
lower note.

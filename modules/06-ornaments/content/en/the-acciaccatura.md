# The acciaccatura: the crushed note

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. VI §§73, 75, pp. 22–26. Original prose.

The **acciaccatura** looks like an appoggiatura with a light stroke
through its stem. Its name means "crushed": it has no time value of its
own, is played as quickly as possible, and is never stressed — the
stress stays on the main note. That is the reliable way to tell the two
apart, Gehrkens says: the appoggiatura is always accented, the
acciaccatura never. Older books called it the short appoggiatura.

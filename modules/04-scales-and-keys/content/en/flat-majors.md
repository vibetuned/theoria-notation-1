# The flat major keys, written two ways

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. VII sec. 83 and its note, pp. 30–32. Original prose.

The flat side of the fifteen major keys, in the same two-way picture:
the signature on the upper staff, the scale with its flats written by
each note on the lower one. F major needs one flat, B♭; every further
key adds one more, until C♭ major flattens all seven tones. As each
flat sounds in the scale, its twin lights in the signature.

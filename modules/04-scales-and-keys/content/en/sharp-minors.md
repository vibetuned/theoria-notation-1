# The sharp minor keys, three forms each

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. VIII secs. 88–89, pp. 34–35. Original prose.

Every minor key borrows the signature of its relative major, and that
signature describes the primitive form only. Here are A minor and the
seven sharp minor keys, each drawn twice — signature above, scale
below — and each played in its three forms. The raised 7th (harmonic)
and raised 6th (melodic) are always written as accidentals in the
scale; the signature never changes. In the keys with many sharps a
raised note may need a double sharp.

# The flat minor keys, three forms each

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. VIII secs. 88–89, pp. 34–35. Original prose.

The seven flat minor keys, D minor through A♭ minor, in the same
two-way picture and the same three forms. On the flat side, raising a
tone often means taking a flat away rather than adding a sharp: in C
minor the harmonic 7th is B, not B♭, and the melodic 6th is A, not A♭.
Either way the change lives in the scale, never in the signature.

# The key signature

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. III §§22–24, pp. 8–9. Original prose.

When a piece uses the same sharps or flats all the way through, they
are written once, right after the clef at the start of every line: the
**key signature**. A sharp in the signature applies to every note with
that letter, in every octave, for the whole piece, unless an accidental
says otherwise for one measure. The page gets lighter and the sound
does not change at all.

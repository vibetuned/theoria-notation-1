# One signature, two keys

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. III §23, pp. 8–9 (Figs. 12–13). Original prose.

Every key signature is shared by two keys: a major key and its
**relative minor**. One sharp is the signature of G major and of E
minor. To tell which one a piece is in, Gehrkens offers a simple test:
look at the last chord, and above all its bass note. A piece that ends
on the G chord (DO, in movable-do singing) is in G major; one that ends
on the E chord (LA) is almost certainly in E minor.

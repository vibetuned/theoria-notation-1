# D.S. al Coda: the sign and the coda

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. IV §39, pp. 13–16. Original prose.

*Dal segno* — D.S. — means "from the sign": go back, not to the
beginning, but to the **segno**. A **coda** ("tail") is a closing
section set apart at the end. *D.S. al Coda* reads: go back to the
sign, play until *To Coda*, then jump to the coda. The first time
through you pass *To Coda* without jumping. Gehrkens writes the same
journey from the beginning as *D.C. e poi la coda* ("from the head, and
then the coda").

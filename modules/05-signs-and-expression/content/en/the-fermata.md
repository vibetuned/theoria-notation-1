# The fermata: hold this note

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. IV §44, pp. 13–16. Original prose.

The **fermata** (or *hold*), an arc with a dot under it, stops the beat
for a moment: the note under it is held longer than its written value.
How much longer is not written anywhere — Gehrkens leaves it to the
character of the music and the taste of the player or conductor. Over a
bar line, the same sign marks a short pause between two sections.

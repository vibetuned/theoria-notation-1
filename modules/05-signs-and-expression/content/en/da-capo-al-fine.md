# D.C. al Fine: back to the beginning

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. IV §39, pp. 13–16. Original prose.

*Da capo* — D.C. — means "from the head": go back to the beginning of
the piece. The word **Fine** ("end") marks where the repeated part
stops, so *D.C. al Fine* reads: back to the start, and play until Fine.
Gehrkens notes that composers once used a fermata over the bar line for
the same purpose; today the word Fine is the usual sign.

# Repeat signs and first and second endings

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. IV §§36–37, pp. 13–16. Original prose.

Two dots in front of a double bar line form a **repeat sign**: go back
and play the passage again. If there is no matching sign facing the
other way, go back to the very beginning; if there is one, repeat only
the music between the two. Often the repeat should end differently the
second time. Brackets over the last bars mark the **first ending** (play
it the first time, then repeat) and the **second ending** (skip the
first ending the second time and play this instead).

# 8va and 8vb: an octave higher or lower

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. IV §§45–46, pp. 13–16. Original prose.

Very high or very low notes need so many ledger lines that they are
hard to read. Instead, write them an octave closer to the staff and add
an **octave sign**: *8va* (*all'ottava*) above the notes means play an
octave higher than written; *8vb* (Gehrkens: *8va bassa*) below the
notes means an octave lower. A dashed line shows how far the sign
reaches. Where it ends, notes sound as written again — older music
sometimes says so with the word *loco*, "in place".

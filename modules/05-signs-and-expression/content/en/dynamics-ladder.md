# From ppp to fff: the dynamics ladder

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XIII §120, pp. 56–61. Original prose.

How loud to play is written with letters taken from Italian words: **p**
for *piano* (soft) and **f** for *forte* (loud). An **m** for *mezzo*
(medium) softens either one: *mp* is medium soft, *mf* medium loud.
Doubling or tripling a letter pushes it further: *pp* very soft, *ppp*
as soft as possible, *ff* very loud, *fff* as loud as possible. Gehrkens
stresses that these marks are relative, never absolute: *forte* only
means louder than *piano* — the music, the instrument and the room
decide the rest.

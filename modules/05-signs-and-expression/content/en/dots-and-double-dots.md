# Dots after notes: half again, and half of that

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. V §§49–51, pp. 17–21. Original prose.

A **dot** after a note makes it half again as long: a half note lasts 2
beats, so a dotted half lasts 2 + 1 = 3. A **second dot** adds half of
what the first dot added — here half a beat — so a double-dotted half
lasts 3½ beats, and an eighth note fills the rest of a 4/4 bar.
Gehrkens states the same rule as fractions: one dot makes three halves
of the plain value, two dots seven quarters, and the rare third dot
fifteen eighths.

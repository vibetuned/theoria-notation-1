# Signs and expression

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), chs. IV–V pp. 13–21 and ch. XIII pp. 56–61. Original prose; the
figures are the book's own (`assets/book/fig25-27.musicxml`,
`fig30-33.musicxml`).

The connective tissue first. The **tie** welds two notes of one pitch
into a single sound, even across a barline. The **slur** looks similar
and means four different things by context: play *legato*; mark a
phrase; carry one syllable through many notes; or bracket a tuplet.
**Dot arithmetic** is exact — one dot makes 3/2 of the value, two 7/4,
three 15/8 — and the articulation marks (staccato dot, tenuto dash,
accent) govern how a tone is left as much as how it is struck.

**Dynamics** are relative, never absolute: the ladder *ppp* through
*fff* orders loudness, and three swells shape it — *sforzando* on one
tone, *rinforzando* lifting a passage at once, *crescendo* growing by
degrees. Finally the **roadmap**: repeat dots, first and second endings,
*D.C.*, *D.S.* with its segno, *fine*, the fermata — and the octave
signs *8va*, *8va bassa*, *loco* that fold the staff's range outward.

## Narration (video: DotsTiesSlurs)

1. The tie welds two notes of one pitch into one sound — the only way a
   note outlives its barline.
2. The slur is a chameleon: legato, phrase-mark, one syllable across
   many notes, or a tuplet's bracket. Context decides.
3. Dots are arithmetic: three halves, seven quarters, fifteen eighths of
   the plain value.
4. And the little marks — dot, dash, wedge — decide how a note leaves the
   room.

## Narration (video: LoudSoftAndTheRoadmap)

1. Loudness in music is relative: the ladder from ppp to fff orders it;
   the piece decides what forte means tonight.
2. Three ways to swell: strike one tone (sforzando), lift a passage at
   once (rinforzando), or grow by degrees (crescendo).
3. The roadmap signs steer the journey: repeats, endings, da capo, dal
   segno, fine.
4. And when the staff runs out of room, 8va folds the range up, 8va
   bassa down, and loco brings it home.

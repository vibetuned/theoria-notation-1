# Ties and slurs: one curve, two jobs

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. V §§54–55, pp. 17–21. Original prose.

Two curved lines look almost alike but do different jobs. A **tie**
joins two notes of the *same* pitch: you play the first and hold it
through the second, so the two values add up to one sound — the only
way a single sound can carry on across a bar line. A **slur** arches
over notes of *different* pitches and asks for them to be played
smoothly connected, *legato*. Gehrkens lists other uses of the slur as
well — a phrase mark, one syllable sung on several notes, the bracket of
a triplet — but "play these smoothly" is the one to learn first.

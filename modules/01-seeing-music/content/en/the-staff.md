# The staff: lines, spaces and degrees

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. II §§12–14, p. 5. Original prose.

Music is written on a **staff** of five lines, counted from the bottom
up. Between the lines lie four spaces, and the staff also owns the
space just below its bottom line and the space just above its top line:
five lines and six spaces in all. Each line and each space is one
**degree** of the staff. The rule for reading it is the rule of a map:
the higher a note sits on the staff, the higher it sounds.

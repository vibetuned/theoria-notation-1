# Head, stem, flag: what a note shows

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. I §1 p. 1, ch. III §§28–31 pp. 10–11. Original prose.

A note has up to three parts: a **head**, a **stem**, and a **flag**
(Gehrkens calls it the hook). The head's place on the staff gives the
pitch; the note's shape gives its length. A whole note is an open head
with no stem; a half note adds a stem; a quarter note fills the head; an
eighth note adds one flag and a sixteenth two. Each lasts half as long
as the one before. British musicians name the same values semibreve,
minim, crotchet, quaver and semiquaver.

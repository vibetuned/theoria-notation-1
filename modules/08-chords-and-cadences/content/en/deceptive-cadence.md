# The deceptive cadence: V to vi

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVIII sec. 207, p. 91. Original prose; the chords are the
voicing used in this module's cadence worksheets.

After a dominant chord the ear expects the tonic. A **deceptive**
cadence lets V move somewhere else instead — most often to vi, the minor
chord on the 6th degree. The ear is, as the name says, deceived. In the
bass, the 5th degree steps up to the 6th where the 1st was expected.
Like the half cadence, it adds variety in the middle of a piece but
cannot end one.

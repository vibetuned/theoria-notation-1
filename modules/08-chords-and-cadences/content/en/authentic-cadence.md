# The authentic cadence: V to I

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVIII secs. 202–203, p. 89. Original prose; the chords are
the voicing used in this module's cadence worksheets.

A **cadence** is the close of a phrase — in harmony, its last two
chords. The most frequent of all is the dominant chord, V, moving to the
tonic chord, I. Gehrkens calls it the **authentic** cadence because of
its directness. Listen to the bass: it carries the root of each chord,
moving from the 5th degree of the scale to the 1st.

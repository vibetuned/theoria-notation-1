# The plagal cadence: IV to I

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVIII sec. 205, p. 90. Original prose; the chords are the
voicing used in this module's cadence worksheets.

In a **plagal** cadence the tonic chord is approached from the
subdominant, IV, instead of the dominant. It is softer than the
authentic close, and so familiar from the end of hymns that it is also
called the church cadence or the "Amen" cadence. In the bass, the root
of IV — the 4th degree — moves down to the 1st.

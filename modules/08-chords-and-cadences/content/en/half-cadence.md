# The half cadence: stopping on V

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVIII sec. 206, p. 90. Original prose; the chords are the
voicing used in this module's cadence worksheets.

A phrase does not have to end at home. In a **half cadence** it comes to
rest on the dominant chord, V, reached from the tonic chord. The music
pauses — a comma rather than a full stop — and asks for another phrase
to follow, which is why a half cadence can never close a whole piece. In
the bass, the 1st degree moves to the 5th.

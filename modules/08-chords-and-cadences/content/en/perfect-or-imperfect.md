# Perfect or imperfect? Watch the soprano

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVIII secs. 204–205, pp. 89–90 (fig. 68). Original prose.

The same authentic cadence can be voiced in several ways, and Gehrkens
names them by the highest voice — the soprano — of the final chord.
When it sings the **root** of I, the cadence is a **perfect** authentic
cadence, the most satisfying close to the ear. When it sings the third
or the fifth, the cadence is **imperfect**. The plagal cadence is named
by the same rule.

# Rhythm, measure, tempo: three kinds of "time"

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. X §97 p. 44, ch. XI §§104–107 pp. 48–49. Original prose.

Musicians use the one word "time" for three different things, and
Gehrkens asks us to keep them apart. **Rhythm** lives in the music
itself: the pattern of long and short sounds and where their accents
fall. **Measure** is the way we group the beats, marked off by bar lines
and named by the time signature; Gehrkens would say "three-quarter
measure" where most musicians today say "3/4 time". **Tempo** is the
speed of the beats. Play the same rhythm in the same measure faster, and
only the tempo has changed.

# Slowing down and speeding up

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XII §§111–113, pp. 53–54. Original prose.

Some tempo words change the speed along the way, and Gehrkens sorts
them by how they do it. **Ritardando** (rit.) and **rallentando**
(rall.) slow down little by little, each beat a bit longer than the
last; **accelerando** (accel.) speeds up little by little. **Meno
mosso** ("less motion") is slower at once, and **più mosso** ("more
motion") faster at once. **A tempo** returns to the speed before the
change. **Rubato**, "robbed time", takes a little time from some notes
and gives it to others.

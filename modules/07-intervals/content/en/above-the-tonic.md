# Above the tonic: major and perfect intervals

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVII sec. 194, pp. 85–86 (fig. 64). Original prose.

Write out a major scale and measure every tone up from the key-tone:
the result is a reference set with no surprises. The 2nd, 3rd, 6th and
7th are all **major**; the prime (Gehrkens' word for two tones on the
same degree), the 4th, the 5th and the octave are all **perfect**.
Nothing measured up from the tonic is minor or diminished — those are
what you get when one of these is made smaller.

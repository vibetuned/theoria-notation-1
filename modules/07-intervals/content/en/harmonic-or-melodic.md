# Harmonic and melodic intervals

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVII sec. 183, p. 83 (figs. 62–63). Original prose.

An **interval** is the relation between two tones in pitch. Sound the
two at the same moment and the interval is **harmonic**; sound them one
after the other and it is **melodic**. Gehrkens shows it with the same
pair both ways — F and A — because nothing about the interval itself
changes: it is a third either way, only the manner of sounding differs.

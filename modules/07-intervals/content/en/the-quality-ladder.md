# Larger and smaller: the quality ladder

**Source**: Gehrkens, Music Notation and Terminology (A. S. Barnes,
1914), ch. XVII secs. 187, 189 and 194, pp. 84–86. Original prose.

Every interval number has a ladder of sizes, one half step per rung,
while the letters stay put. For thirds (and seconds, sixths, sevenths)
the rungs run diminished, minor, major, augmented. For fifths (and
primes, fourths, octaves) there is no major or minor: the rung below
perfect is diminished, the rung above augmented. Gehrkens sums it up:
next smaller than major is minor, next smaller than perfect or minor is
diminished, and next larger than major or perfect is augmented.
